## How I Approach Projects

My typical workflow follows these stages:

1. **Problem definition** – Understand the problem and identify the objectives.
2. **Data collection** – Identify and acquire relevant datasets.
3. **Data preparation** – Clean, transform, and validate data.
4. **Exploratory analysis** – Investigate trends, relationships, and patterns.
5. **Modelling** – Apply suitable statistical or machine learning techniques.
6. **Evaluation** – Assess model performance and identify limitations.
7. **Communication** – Present findings through visualizations, reports, or applications.
